About Lumie

Lumie is a modern beauty brand that believes in glowing confidence. We bring you high-quality makeup products inspired by nature and designed to make you feel your best every day.

Founded in 2025, Lumie started with one simple idea — to help every woman discover her natural glow. Our products are cruelty-free, skin-friendly, and made with care.
